### Chapter II

Every program tells a story, and the clearer that story is, the easier it becomes for others to understand.maintain and extend it.



Narrative code follows recurring plot patterns such as Delivering, Cleaning, Archiving, Defense and Transformation

4 Narrative levels:

- Actions are the simplest narrative elements. They represent small, individual events, such as walking, eating or drinking, and server as the basic 
building bock of the story

- Scenes group a few actions together to form a meaningful moment. A scene unfolds in a specific time and place and leas to a distinct outcome. A scene-level
method should usually embody one dominat plot, keeping responsabilities focudes

- Chapters: consist of multiple scenes. They represent major narrative units that advance the larget arc of the history. At this level, more than one plot
may be present. but they must remain aligned around single coherent goal.
